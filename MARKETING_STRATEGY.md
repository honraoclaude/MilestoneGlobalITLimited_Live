## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Do not try to sell to "any UK SME." That is too vague to create effective messaging. Instead, focus on businesses that have **all four** of these traits:

| Trait | What it means |
|---|---|
| **5–30 employees** | Small enough that admin pain is visible; large enough to afford £1,500 |
| **10+ hours/week of manual repetitive work** | The ROI maths works; they can feel the pain |
| **Customer-facing business** | Enquiries, bookings, onboarding — AI delivers immediate, visible results |
| **Owner / Director makes decisions** | Short sales cycle, no procurement committee |

**Best-fit industries (start with one, not all):**

| Industry | Pain | AI Solution | Why they pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Estate agents / letting agents | Drowning in WhatsApp/email enquiries 24/7 | Chatbot triages, books viewings | Every missed enquiry is a missed listing |
| Dental / medical practices | Phone tag for appointments, no-shows | 24/7 booking bot, reminder automation | No-shows cost £50–200 each |
| Accountancy firms | Manual client onboarding, chasing documents | Onboarding automation, document collection | Partner time is billable at £200+/hr |
| Law firms | Repetitive client intake, follow-up | Intake automation, status updates | Same — partner time is expensive |
| Trade businesses (plumbers, electricians) | Missed calls while on jobs | AI answers, qualifies, books | Every missed call is £200–500 job |

**Recommended first niche: Estate agents / letting agents**
- Easy to find (Rightmove, Zoopla, Google)
- Clear pain (missed enquiries = missed commission)
- 2–10 person offices are common
- Can afford £1,500 easily (one extra let = that paid for)
- Decision maker is usually the owner, reachable on LinkedIn

## 3. Positioning

**Current headline:** "Your Business Deserves Smarter AI Agents"
This is broad. As the company gets its first clients, sharpen this to something like:
> *"The AI automation company for UK service businesses that can't afford to miss a single enquiry"*

**Competitive positioning:**

| Alternative | Why clients might consider them | Why you win |
|---|---|---|
| Zapier / Make (DIY) | Cheaper upfront | You do it for them — no technical knowledge needed |
| Big consultancies (Accenture, KPMG) | Credibility | 100x cheaper, 10x faster, personal relationship |
| Offshore dev shop | Even cheaper | UK-based, GDPR compliant, your timezone, you can meet them |
| Generic IT company | "One stop shop" | AI specialist — this is all you do |

**Your unfair advantage:** You are a solo founder who is both salesperson and technical delivery — so your overhead is zero, your speed is fast, and your quality of attention is higher than any agency.

## 4. Messaging Framework

Use this to write LinkedIn posts, cold emails, and website copy.

**Core problem to agitate:**
> *"Every hour your team spends on emails, booking calls, and admin is an hour not spent on the work that actually grows your business — and every enquiry that sits unanswered for 4 hours is a customer who already emailed your competitor."*

**The transformation you sell (not "AI chatbots"):**
- Before: 4-hour response times, missed calls, 15 hrs/week of admin
- After: Replies in 30 seconds, 24/7, staff doing real work instead of inbox management

**3 messages that convert for B2B:**
1. **ROI** — "A typical letting agent saves £18,000/year in staff time after we automate their enquiry handling."
2. **Speed** — "We go from discovery call to live deployment in 2–4 weeks."
3. **Risk removal** — "Free 30-minute consultation, no obligation, no jargon."

## 5. Channel Strategy — Prioritised for Zero Budget

### Priority 1 — Start immediately (Week 1)

**LinkedIn Personal Profile** *(highest ROI channel for B2B, zero cost)*
- Your personal profile > company page. People buy from people.
- Optimise your headline to: *"Helping UK service businesses automate enquiries & save 10+ hours/week | Milestone Global IT"*
- Your About section: Tell the story of WHY you started this — not your CV. "I noticed UK SMEs were losing thousands every month to slow customer response and admin overload. So I built an AI company to fix it."
- Post **3× per week** using the content types below
- Connect with **20 target prospects per day** (search: "director" OR "owner" + "estate agent" + "UK")
- Engage (comment) on 5 posts per day from target customers before posting your own

**Direct outreach on LinkedIn** *(fastest route to first client)*

Template that works:
> *"Hi [Name] — I was looking at [Company] and noticed you handle a lot of [viewings/client enquiries/bookings]. I've been helping letting agents automate this so they never miss an enquiry, even at 11pm. Happy to show you a 5-minute demo — no cost, no pitch. Would that be useful?"*

Rules:
- Personalise every message (mention their company or a specific detail)
- Never paste a wall of text
- Do **not** attach a brochure or link in the first message
- Target: 10 personalised messages per day

---

### Priority 2 — Start Week 2

**Cold email outreach**
- Find emails with Hunter.io (free: 25 searches/month) or find them on the company's website
- Email subject lines that get opened: *"Quick question about [Company's] enquiry handling"* or *"Saw your Google reviews — one thing stood out"*
- Keep the email to 5 lines max. The goal is a reply, not a sale.
- Target: 20 emails/day

**Upwork / PeoplePerHour** *(get first clients fast, even at lower rates)*
- Post your services: "AI Chatbot for Small Business UK" and "Workflow Automation Consultant UK"
- Accept a first project at 50–60% of your normal rate just to get a real review
- One 5-star review on Upwork is worth more than any marketing right now

---

### Priority 3 — Month 2+

**Blog / SEO** *(long game — compounding returns)*
Target these searches (low competition, buyer intent):
- "AI chatbot for letting agents UK"
- "automate appointment booking dental practice"
- "how to reduce admin time small business UK"
- "AI answering service for tradespeople UK"

Write one post per week, 800–1,200 words, targeting one keyword. This takes 3–6 months to pay off but becomes free inbound traffic forever.

**Referral partnerships**
- Contact 10 web designers or web agencies: *"I add AI automation to the websites you build. Happy to pay a 10–15% referral fee."*
- Contact 5 business coaches or accountants: same offer
- These partners already have trusted relationships with your exact target customer

**Community presence** *(relationship, not pitching)*
- Reddit: r/UKsmallbusiness — answer questions about automation, AI tools, time management. Mention your company only when directly relevant.
- Facebook: UK Small Business Owners group — same approach
- LinkedIn groups: Search "UK small business" and "UK entrepreneurs"
- Never post a promotional link. Provide value first, every time.

## 6. Content Calendar — LinkedIn

**Post rotation (3 posts/week):**

| Post type | Format | Example topic |
|---|---|---|
| **Educational** | Text or carousel | "5 tasks every estate agent should automate (and how much time they waste)" |
| **Story / Before-After** | Text, 200–400 words | "A plumber was missing 8 calls a day while on jobs. Here's what happened after 3 weeks" |
| **Myth-busting** | Short text, punchy | "AI automation is not just for Amazon. Here's what it costs for a 5-person business" |
| **Authentic / founder** | Personal text | "Day 14 of building a UK AI company from scratch. Here's what I've learned" |
| **Demo / video** | 60-second screen recording | "I built this WhatsApp AI bot for a letting agent in 2 weeks — watch it handle an enquiry" |
| **Question** | Short + poll | "How many hours a week does your team spend on tasks that feel like they should be automated?" |

**Golden rule:** Every post ends with a soft CTA — not "buy my service" but "DM me if you want to see how this works for your business."

## 7. Sales Process

```
Prospect contacts you OR you reach out
        ↓
30-min discovery call (free, no pressure)
  Ask: What's your biggest time drain?
  Ask: How many enquiries/calls per week?
  Ask: What happens when you don't respond fast enough?
  Show: ROI Calculator with their actual numbers
        ↓
Send proposal within 24 hours (1 page, not 20)
  Include: Problem summary, solution, timeline, price, guarantee
        ↓
50% deposit to start
        ↓
2–6 weeks: Build & show demos at key milestones
        ↓
Deploy + handover + 30 days support included
        ↓
Week 5: Ask for testimonial + referral
```

**Discovery call script (key questions):**
1. *"Tell me what a typical day looks like for your team around customer enquiries / bookings."*
2. *"How many hours a week would you say go into just responding to messages and admin?"*
3. *"What happens to an enquiry that comes in at 7pm on a Friday?"*
4. *"If you could get those hours back, what would you do with them?"*

Let them sell themselves. Your job is to listen, then show them the ROI Calculator with their numbers on screen.

## 10. The Single Most Important Rule

**Focus on outreach before content.** It takes 3–6 months for blog posts and LinkedIn content to compound into inbound leads. Right now, the fastest path to revenue is direct, personalised outreach — one human to one human.

Your target is this: **talk to 10 potential clients this week.** Not write a post. Not redesign the website. Talk to 10 people who have the problem you solve.

One conversation → one demo → one client → one case study → everything else gets easier.
